How a route is made

6 stages · 4 routes
1

Rule compile

20 m raster in a frame rotated along the bee-line. Every rule becomes a tower-forbidden, angle-tower-forbidden or conductor-forbidden mask, a ₹/m² corridor cost, a crossing-direction raster or a foundation multiplier. Hill ground from the 30 m DEM.

2

GPU search

Pylon graph: sites every 50 m along and across, spans 100 m to the design span, state = site + incoming span, tower type from the deflection band. Exact dynamic programme ordered by chainage; no backward detours.

3

25 m refinement

The search again on a 25 m lattice, confined to 300 m either side of the 50 m solution. Kept when feasible and no dearer.

4

Forest cap

A strict route over 5 ha of reserve forest is re-searched with forest priced up, by bisection on the multiplier up to ×1000, until it sits under the cap. Reported cost uses true prices.

5

Tower spotting

String-pulling to angle points, exact vector re-check of every rule, 10 m cost programme along each leg, crossing towers into their exact window, section points, DEM clearance with +18 m extensions, angle-point merges and descent, single-tower polish.

6

Log and export

Typed decision events with source and confidence, violations and warnings per tower, DXF / SHP / KML / CSV / PDF and a manifest.

R1

Least cost

Plain objective: towers, line, land, RoW, forest, foundations.

R2

Minimum deviation

Same solve plus ₹40,000 per metre of lateral offset per km. Overlap check waits for R1.

R3

Relaxed

Every "Last resort" rule opened: crossable at a price, towers inside where piles are allowed, approval months reported. Protected areas stay No-Go.

R4

Second least cost

R1's corridor priced up by ₹20,000/m within 150 m when a route shares more than 85 % of it.

Crossing an existing power line

rules power_line · power_line_lt

The most constrained crossing in the set. Preferred at the wire's null point and at 90°; allowed within a band of their span and an angle window; the two crossing towers stand in a distance window from the crossed conductor.

Search-only steering

never in the reported ₹

These pull the search one way or another. The price shown for every route is always the true cost without them.

Stay near existing lines

Spans and towers within 1,000 m of an existing line or tower of 220 kV or more get a 20 % discount on line, RoW and tower price in the search and in spotting. Parallel-spacing and crossing rules still apply.

Avoid hills

Hill ground = relief of at least 25 m within 250 m and mean slope of at least 3° over 270 m, patches of 5 ha or more. Surcharge ₹2 Cr per km of line and ₹1 Cr per tower on it.

Forest cap

Strict routes (R1, R2, R4) may take at most 5 ha of reserve forest. Met by bisection on the forest price multiplier; search surcharge per hectare is 0.

Crossing preferences

A power-line crossing off the null point costs the search up to 20 % of the crossing tower's price, graded from 0 at the null point. Every 25 m a crossing tower stands beyond its window maximum costs 100 % of that price.

Route diversity

A route sharing more than 85 % of its 150 m corridor with an earlier one is re-run with that corridor priced up ₹20,000/m.

Learn from existing lines

Only when a route finds no feasible path: a wetland, pond, lake, reservoir, coastal water, CRZ zone, salt pan, forest, sanctuary or park that existing towers already stand in is opened, that polygon only, and listed with the route. Crossing, built-up, substation, airport, defence, monument and dam rules are never eased.

Known gaps

what cannot move the route today
  • Dams and Nalla rules have no data layer yet.
  • International border is No-Go by decision but has no layer.
  • Crossing-tower deviation (the "n±1" rule) is enforced since 13 Oct 2026: each crossing tower turns at most 45°, in the search and as a spotting violation. The README line calling it a warning is stale.
  • CRZ uses the sea polygon edge in place of the HTL; no CRZ I to IV zones, no clearance cost.
  • Wetland HFL 50 m foundation setback has no high-flood-level data.
  • Cell towers are the 2022 Tarang Sanchar scrape, about 81 % of the real count; no refresh source.
  • Dholera airport has no mapped runway, so the approach funnel cannot fire there.
  • Ground clearance is checked on the 30 m Copernicus DEM, not the 10 m the research asks for.
  • Other voltages than 765 kV: TSD span tables, ground clearance and sag use earlier figures until their sheets arrive.
  • Backward detours and corridor-guided detours round blocks wider than the band are not representable.
  • Values marked ? are best guesses pending AESL confirmation.
