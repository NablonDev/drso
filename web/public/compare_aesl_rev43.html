Method and limits

  • Rev 43 is priced and checked exactly as solved (frozen re-price of solve 105: ₹595.388 Cr, identical to the solve).
  • The AESL line has angle points but no towers. Towers were placed with our leg programme on the 21 legs our rules allow, and evenly at ≤ 431 m on the 37 legs our rules block (431 m keeps straight runs A towers under the adjacent-span table). Types, crossing towers, checks and cost then come from the same engine as rev 43.
  • So the AESL cost and flags are "the AESL alignment under our rules and prices", not AESL's own design: its tower family, tower positions and rule set may differ, and its flags include features mapped after it was designed.
  • Proximity is measured on the tower polylines (UTM 42N), every 50 m, in both directions.
  • Source files: scratchpad/report/report_data.json, compute.py, build.py.
